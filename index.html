Presentation Overview

Explore the key challenges, system components, and goals of the MarsPath Pro project.

01 Mission Overview

MarsPath Pro: Smart Navigation for a Safer Mars

MarsPath Pro aims to help astronauts explore Mars more safely by combining map information, route planning, and environmental awareness in one integrated navigation concept.

Plan

Identify routes and important locations before and during exploration.

Analyze

Consider terrain conditions and potential environmental risks.

02 Environmental Challenges

Why Is Surface Navigation Critical?

Communication Delays

Radio communication between Earth and Mars can take several minutes one way, so astronauts cannot always rely on immediate guidance.

Harsh Environment

Mars has a thin atmosphere, extreme temperature changes, and challenging surface conditions.

Dust Storms

Dust can reduce visibility and affect equipment and energy systems.

Safe Decisions

The system should help astronauts understand risks and choose safer next steps.

03 System Overview

One Integrated Navigation Concept

MarsPath Pro brings relevant information together to support a clearer picture of the surrounding area.

Terrain Mapping

View surface features, slopes, and important landmarks.

Route Planning

Review possible paths and compare their conditions.

Risk Awareness

Highlight areas that may require extra attention.

Mission Support

Present useful information in a clear and accessible way.

04 Smart Route Planning

Choose a More Informed Path

The navigation concept is designed to compare route options using terrain information and known hazards. Astronauts can review alternatives and make decisions based on the available data.

Route Comparison

Compare distance, terrain difficulty, and potential obstacles.

Adaptive Guidance

Reconsider the route when new information or changing conditions become available.

05 Path Brain

The Intelligence Behind the Route

Path Brain represents the analysis and decision-support layer of MarsPath Pro. It organizes incoming information and helps turn it into understandable navigation guidance.

Collect

Bring together map, terrain, and environmental information.

Evaluate

Review route conditions and identify possible concerns.

Guide

Present clear route options and supporting information.

Update

Refresh recommendations when relevant data changes.

06 Mission Resources

Supporting a Sustainable Mission

Long-duration exploration depends on careful awareness of essential resources and supporting systems.

Food Production

Consider the role of controlled food-growing systems in long-term missions.

Water Recycling

Recognize water recovery and reuse as important mission-support functions.

Oxygen Management

Account for life-support systems that provide breathable air.

Energy Control

Consider power availability when planning activities and routes.

07 Project Goals

Explore · Survive · Thrive

MarsPath Pro aims to support safer and better-informed exploration by connecting navigation, environmental awareness, and mission information in one clear concept.

Safer Exploration

Help astronauts recognize potential hazards and understand their surroundings.

Better Decisions

Make useful information easier to review when communication with Earth is delayed.
